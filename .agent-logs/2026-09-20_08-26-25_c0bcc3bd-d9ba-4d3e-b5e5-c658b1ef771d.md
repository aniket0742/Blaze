## 5. Remaining issues and known limitations

**Found and fixed during QA:** the server-rendered HTML contained **no account control at all**. `AccountMenu` is a client island that renders an empty box until `/api/session` resolves, so with JavaScript off there was no way to sign in from the header and **no way to sign out anywhere in the app**. I did not change the header architecture — keeping session state out of the root layout is exactly what preserves the 218 prerendered pages, and the blank-until-loaded state deliberately avoids flashing "Sign in" at someone already signed in. Instead I added the two additive fallbacks in §1. A side benefit: sign-out is now verifiable over HTTP, which it previously wasn't.

**Two of my initial QA assertions were wrong, not the app** — I asserted the badge should exclude stale/out-of-stock lines, but DECISIONS records the opposite ("the badge counts what is in the cart; the subtotal counts what is orderable"), and one check missed `&amp;` escaping. Corrected the tests, not the behaviour.

**Unchanged by design:** stock is validated but never decremented; delivery is always free; order status is always `placed`; reviews are read-only; no FK to `auth.users`; light theme only. All are in the README's limitations table with reasons.
